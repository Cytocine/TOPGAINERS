Uses the Style and Universe above. Day trade: top opening gappers graded at 10:30 ET. Overnight: top gainers graded at 15:45 ET, held to the next close. Every trade uses the same stop / 2R target the app shows.
How the grade works
  • Day trade rewards same-time-of-day RVOL, price above VWAP, 30m EMA9>EMA21 trend, a break of the 15-min opening range / premarket high, strength vs SPY, staying near the highs, and not being stretched from VWAP (measured in daily ATRs).
  • Overnight rewards a close in the top of the day's range, RVOL, a close above VWAP, a daily uptrend, a new / near 20-day high (clean air), and a healthy 0.5–2 ATR move. A 3+ ATR move is treated as blow-off risk.
  • Grades are A 80+, B 65–79, C 50–64. Caps keep weak setups from grading high: thin volume or below VWAP caps at C; RVOL under 1.5×, a risk-off tape, fading volume, a downtrend, or a 3+ ATR run caps at B.
  • Sector strength (S&P 500 names only): the sector's ETF ranks vs SPY today. Top-3 sectors above VWAP add 5 points, bottom-3 sectors subtract 5.
  • Stops / targets are ATR-based with a 2R target. Size = risk budget ÷ stop distance (overnight uses the size multiplier above).
  • These weights are sensible defaults, not a proven edge. Log trades from the chart — the log records each grade so you can see whether A's really beat C's over time.
  • Gemini review sends up to 10 hits (C or better in either style) with their metrics and both risk boxes. It answers DAY / OVERNIGHT / PASS with a 1–10 conviction and may name one best trade — or none. It must use the app's stop and target, and with Search grounding on it also looks for earnings and news. It's an opinion on the app's own numbers, not an edge: the trade log records its verdict so you can check whether its picks earn their keep.
  • The app can't see earnings dates. Add any name reporting tonight/tomorrow to the skip list before an overnight hold.
